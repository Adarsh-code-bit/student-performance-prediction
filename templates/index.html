Why is attendance kept separate from academic prediction?

Regulatory Policy vs. Cognitive Capability

Attendance below 75% is an administrative regulation, not a mathematical prediction of intellectual ability. Conflating regulatory compliance with academic capability produces severe algorithmic bias, obscuring capable students who missed class due to personal emergencies while hiding academic struggles of regular attendees.

Why Decision Trees Instead of Neural Networks?

University academic decisions have significant consequences on students' lives and career pathways. Faculty advisors, mentors, and students need transparent, explainable logic they can inspect and understand. A Decision Tree provides a step-by-step logic trail that can be audited and discussed openly.
